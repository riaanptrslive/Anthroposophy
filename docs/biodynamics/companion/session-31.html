Source commentary · PDF capture 118

Seed-ash counterforce

Inspect this page’s inventory

Burning weed seeds is proposed to produce a counterforce to the fertility associated with lunar activity. Repeated scattering is assigned a four-year result. Dandelion illustrates the distinction between useful cultivation in one place and suppression elsewhere. Steiner invokes small-dose research, but the passage's most important qualification concerns what testing means to him: he is inwardly certain and regards external verification chiefly as a compromise needed for public acceptance. This is stronger than a tentative hypothesis offered for possible rejection. A fair lesson teaches both the invitation to experiment and the assertion that spiritual truth needs no such confirmation. The companion analyzes the proposal without asking students to perform it.

Source commentary · PDF capture 119

Arithmetic/workforce analogy

Inspect this page’s inventory

The workforce analogy distinguishes an ideal relation from circumstances interfering with its realization: three times the workers might not produce three times the goods if they spend their time talking. Steiner uses this to explain why a contrary experimental result would not, in his view, defeat an inwardly certain truth. The difficulty is that agricultural efficacy is not a simple arithmetic identity; a test needs criteria for distinguishing an interfering condition from an incorrect claim. His account of mouse control then criticizes harsh methods and externally imposed uniformity. Neighboring populations explain why a single farm's effort might be limited, preparing his social argument for shared understanding.

Source commentary · PDF capture 120

Shared understanding versus police

Inspect this page’s inventory

Steiner prefers cooperation based on insight to enforcement by police. The mouse proposal links skin ash with Venus against the Scorpion, extending the plant-focused planetary discussion to zodiacal conditions for animals. He says animals retain lunar forces and are therefore less dependent on the immediate lunar phase; other planetary relationships still matter. Wide but imperfect reach explains why untreated neighboring areas remain relevant. The method is presented confidently, while the final appeal distinguishes knowledge from inherited superstition. That distinction states the authority he claims for spiritual research; it does not independently establish the method's efficacy. The course keeps this as a historical proposal, not an animal-treatment exercise.

Read the explained answer

He treats it as a reason to examine circumstances while preserving confidence in the inward truth. That stance differs from treating efficacy as a hypothesis that a well-designed failure might overturn.
