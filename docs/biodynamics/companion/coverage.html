Omissions, qualifications and deferred material

  • All 135 capture pages have an inventory entry and teaching commentary. Every identified highlighted idea has a dedicated explanation and an audit link. Page coverage does not mean every printed sentence is reproduced.
  • Figure 7’s conceptual structure and the dates discussed in the surrounding prose are taught. A reliable transcription and independent reconciliation of every small handwritten date and alternative layer label in that diagram remains deferred; no such details have been guessed.
  • The bibliography is covered as a reading map. The contents of all its listed books, and the complete source works behind Courtney’s nested citations, have not been independently read or verified in this task.
  • The full GA 136, GA 230 and GA 327 cycles are outside this anthology. Later orchard, vineyard, livestock and other discussions promised within the original courses are not silently reconstructed.
  • The scientific comparisons address specific disagreements. They are not a systematic review of all biodynamic farming trials, preparation efficacy or calendar research.
  • The new detailed companion is in English. The existing nine-lesson course remains available in English and Brazilian Portuguese; a complete Portuguese translation of this expansion is not included.
  • Page references are PDF captures. The Kindle locations recorded for highlights identify the displayed capture start, not the exact beginning of each highlighted sentence.
