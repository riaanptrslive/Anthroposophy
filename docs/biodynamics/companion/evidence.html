Spiritual claims and scientific comparisons

Keep the explanation of a claim separate from the evidence for accepting it.

The commentary reconstructs the book’s spiritual and philosophical claims. Instructor examples explain reasoning. The comparisons below identify specific established findings or historical corrections with external sources checked September 17, 2026. They do not constitute a systematic review of biodynamic agriculture.

E1 · Earthquakes

USGS explains fault-related earthquakes through sudden slip and release of accumulated stress. The book supplies no empirical bridge from human passions or preparation use to this mechanism. Its spiritual account is not established earthquake science.

USGS: What causes earthquakes?

Relevant commentary: PDF 22 · PDF 23 · PDF 29

E2 · Contrails

Contrails consist of ice crystals formed in aircraft exhaust under suitable atmospheric conditions. Their persistence or disappearance depends on those conditions; an observation above a farm does not by itself establish a preparation effect.

FAA: Contrails

Relevant commentary: PDF 26

E3 · Flowering-plant fertilization

In flowering plants, fertilization occurs within the ovule after pollen-mediated delivery of sperm; the resulting seed does not first need to enter soil for fertilization. Steiner’s underground account is therefore a substantive disagreement, not just alternative imagery.

OpenStax Biology: Angiosperms

Relevant commentary: PDF 62 · PDF 63 · PDF 64 · PDF 66

E4 · Microbes and soil life

Soil organisms actively contribute to decomposition and nutrient cycling. This differs from treating microbes only as signs of an underlying condition. Acknowledging active microbes does not mean every commercial inoculant will be effective.

USDA NRCS: Soil Biology Primer

Relevant commentary: PDF 80 · PDF 84 · PDF 97

E5 · Nitrogen fixation is not transmutation

Biological nitrogen fixation converts existing atmospheric nitrogen into ammonia through prokaryotic activity. It does not turn potassium or calcium into nitrogen. Changing elemental identity concerns the nucleus; the lecture gives no evidence establishing that process in compost.

OpenStax Biology: Nutritional adaptations of plants · OpenStax Chemistry: Transmutation

Relevant commentary: PDF 106

E6 · Sound propagation

Sound in air propagates as pressure disturbances through the gaseous medium. Water vapor is not a prerequisite for hearing sound. Courtney’s water/tone-ether correspondence must be distinguished from this physical explanation.

OpenStax Physics: Sound

Relevant commentary: PDF 24

E7 · Digestion and bodily substance

Digestion breaks food into nutrients that are absorbed and used for energy, growth and repair. This differs from the account that largely assigns bodily substance to refined sensory/atmospheric uptake. Historical protein quantities and therapeutic remarks in the book are not dietary or medical guidance.

NIDDK: Your digestive system and how it works

Relevant commentary: PDF 74 · PDF 78 · PDF 79 · PDF 112

E8 · Livestock and poisonous plants

Livestock can be poisoned by plants, including material in forage. Instinct is not a dependable guarantee of safety. This directly limits the lecture’s universal reassurance that animals avoid what harms them.

University of Minnesota Extension: Plants poisonous to livestock · Extension: Feeding weedy forages

Relevant commentary: PDF 110

E9 · Plant disease

For infectious plant disease, the disease triangle relates a susceptible host, a pathogen and a conducive environment. This gives a specific biological account of disease conditions, separate from the book’s astral-body definition and lunar mediation account.

University of Minnesota: Plant diseases and disorders

Relevant commentary: PDF 75 · PDF 105 · PDF 123 · PDF 124

E10 · Animal classifications

Fishes and amphibians are vertebrate groups, and nematodes are roundworms rather than insects. These biological distinctions should remain visible when studying the book’s idealized complements and its loose grouping of lower animals.

OpenStax Biology: Fishes · OpenStax Biology: Superphylum Ecdysozoa

Relevant commentary: PDF 67 · PDF 68 · PDF 71 · PDF 121 · PDF 122 · PDF 126

E11 · The 1945 chronology

The first nuclear explosion, Trinity, occurred on July 16, 1945. Courtney’s April 1945 association must not be taught as the date of that event. Historical chronology and Powell’s spiritual chronology require separate evaluation.

US Department of Energy: Trinity site
