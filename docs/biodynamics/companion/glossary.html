Terms in context

Definitions follow this book’s use. They explain a framework without assuming that its spiritual entities are scientifically established.

Anthroposophy / spiritual science

Steiner’s proposed disciplined investigation of spiritual reality. In this book the name marks a claimed method and worldview, not an automatic equivalence with natural science.

Farm individuality

A farm conceived as an internally related living whole, understood by Courtney in relation to a living Earth and cosmos.

PDF 7 · PDF 12

Etheric

The formative life principle in the source’s constitution scheme; not a synonym for a chemical nutrient or an established physical energy.

Astral

The principle of sentience, desire and inward activity in humans and animals; also used for ordering influences in the wider natural world.

PDF 47 · PDF 49 · PDF 82

I / ego

The individual center of selfhood and continuity. In the ethical passages it requires truthful memory and conscience, not self-importance.

PDF 50 · PDF 51 · PDF 52

Elementals

Spiritual beings assigned activities in earth, water, air and warmth: gnomes, undines, sylphs and fire spirits.

PDF 41 · PDF 46 · PDF 47

Imagination and Inspiration

Technical names for modes of supersensible cognition in Steiner’s system. They must be distinguished from ordinary invention or an inspired mood.

PDF 57 · PDF 70

Moral perception

An inwardly meaningful quality said to emerge when a sensory impression recedes; not simply moral approval or disapproval of an object.

PDF 38 · PDF 39 · PDF 40

Maya

Appearance or imprint of deeper activity. The lecture applies it to sensory forms, forces, laws and perceived purpose.

PDF 54 · PDF 55

Macrocosm

The larger cosmic whole invoked to explain particular earthly processes. An appeal to wider context does not itself establish each proposed influence.

PDF 98 · PDF 125

Formative ethers

Life, chemical/tone, light and warmth activities in the source’s account. Their correspondences organize its explanation of plant development.

PDF 17 · PDF 18 · PDF 64

Subnature

Spiritual counter-realms in Courtney’s interpretation of other sources; not the geological layers of Earth.

PDF 23 · PDF 24 · PDF 25

Preparation / sheath

A specially treated material and, in several cases, an animal container assigned a formative role. Not every preparation uses a sheath.

Homeopathic / dynamic

In these passages, small quantities are said to carry effects exceeding their material contribution. This describes the source’s claim, not established efficacy.

PDF 99 · PDF 101

Potash, lime and silica

Historical substance terms whose meaning must be read locally: potassium-related materials, calcium-related materials and silicon-containing materials. They should not be treated as identical to the pure elements.

Plant sensitivity / soil intelligence

Proposed responsiveness to environmental resources and appropriate processes, not a demonstration of human-like cognition.

PDF 104 · PDF 107

Pepper

The lecture’s term for ash used in historical weed/pest proposals, named for appearance and scattering; not the culinary spice.
