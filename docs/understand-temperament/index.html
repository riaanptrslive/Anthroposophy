Sources and method

Source: Gilbert Childs, Understand Your Temperament!, Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012 edition; copyright 1995. Assigned pages refer to the supplied capture, which has gaps. A complete replacement edition has not been obtained. Explanations, examples and activities are original course material.

The capture has blank pages, interrupted passages and incomplete diagrams. Lessons follow the contents order using the available text; gaps have not been reconstructed. The external link leads to the publisher’s catalogue, not the full text.

We study Gilbert Childs’s interpretation without using it as a personality test, diagnosis or classification by appearance. Understanding the source does not require agreement with all its claims. Practical adaptations are identified as such.

Try a different invitation into a shared task and compare its effect with your first expectation. Attribute the application to Childs.
