How you will learn

Read the explanation first. Study a short passage in context, compare a worked example, then write and revise your own answer. Each chapter has three key takeaways and three questions with explained answers. Allow 25–40 minutes per lesson, plus the assigned book reading; split the longer chapters across two sessions.

The lessons teach the central arguments through selected passages. Use your own copy for the complete chapter. The full book is not hosted here.

Useful preparation

Review physical body, etheric body, astral body and I.

The foundation course provides a broader introduction. No nutrition qualification is assumed.

Edition, voices and page references

Rudolf Steiner, Nutrition: Food, Health and Spiritual Development. Compiled and edited by Christian von Arnim. Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012; translation and selection © 2008. ISBN 9781855842823.

Page references count the 118 pages of the supplied PDF, beginning with its cover. These are screen captures and differ from the Kindle page indicator and other editions. The Markdown transcription has omissions, especially around highlighted text; quotations in these lessons were checked against the PDF images. All twelve chapter openings are present, but this capture is not a verified complete transcription of the printed book.

The introduction and chapter summaries are the editor’s voice. The main extracts are lecture reports; the source note explains that many were not revised by Steiner. Our explanations, examples and activities are original teaching.

English quotations follow the supplied edition. Portuguese quotations are course study translations displayed alongside the English original, not quotations from a published Portuguese edition. Line-break hyphenation has been removed without changing wording.
