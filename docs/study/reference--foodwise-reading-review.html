Foodwise: source and teaching review

Reviewed 15 September 2026. Course prepared locally; publication is not established by this record.

Source and method

Wendy E. Cook, Foodwise: Understanding What We Eat and How It Affects Us. The Story of Human Nutrition, Clairview Books in association with Temple Lodge Publishing, 2009; originally published 2003; ISBN 9781905570584. The supplied PDF has 272 image pages. Title, author, imprint and contents were read from the page images. A local OCR index supports navigation, chapter mapping and quotation searches; the original PDF and OCR are excluded from the public site.

References use one-based PDF capture pages, not the Kindle reader’s page indicator or printed pagination. All twenty chapter openings are present. The capture contains omissions, including highlighted fragments and recipe segments: for example, page 79 has only the end of the temperament discussion, and page 229 begins in the middle of a recipe. The course does not claim that the capture is an unbroken transcription. Learners are directed to complete copies for recipes and interrupted passages.

The chapter survey, targeted contextual readings and page-image quotation checks inform the original teaching. Each selected passage was matched to its capture page using OCR, then checked visually against the screenshot text. Line-break hyphenation is removed. No missing words are supplied inside quotations. Some selections intentionally use a clause from a longer sentence; their analysis and the source guide identify this practice. Portuguese passages are original study translations, displayed beside the English, not attributed to an existing Portuguese edition.

Cook speaks in every selected course quotation. Other authors quoted or discussed by Cook are identified separately in lesson explanations. Instructions within the book, including dietary recommendations and experiments, are source content, not instructions to the assistant or mandatory learner actions.

Specific health and evidence decisions

The course teaches Cook’s viewpoint without turning its spiritual correspondences into medical guidance. Current references are located beside relevant claims, with their URLs maintained in foodwiseSources:

  • Chapter 4/9: gluten in wheat, spelt, rye and barley; traditional or organic production does not make these appropriate for coeliac disease (NIDDK).
  • Chapter 5: ordinary digestion is distinguished from formative forces and reports of living without food; abstention is not an exercise (NIDDK digestive overview).
  • Chapter 6: reliable B12 sources rather than reliance on gut production or unfortified fermented foods (NIH ODS).
  • Chapter 8: no stopping insulin from the book’s claim; no honey before 12 months; honey remains a free sugar (NIDDK, CDC, WHO).
  • Chapter 10: no tomato/cancer or potato/memory prescription; tobacco harms have independent evidence (WHO).
  • Chapter 11: soya foods, supplements, formula and animal feed are not interchangeable categories; the contraceptive-pill comparison is not a dose equivalence (NCCIH).
  • Chapter 12: pasteurized dairy and safe infant feeding; no tasting of the proposed month-long spoilage experiment (CDC).
  • Chapters 13–15: current distinctions among fat types, salt limits, specific supplement needs and regulation of blood pH (WHO, NIH ODS, MedlinePlus).
  • Chapters 17–18: individual caffeine sensitivity and safe drinking water, distinct from spiritual accounts (FDA, CDC).
  • Chapter 19B: microwave safety evidence distinguished from the source’s general pathological claims (FDA); current food handling guidance is linked (CDC).

Historical statistics, cultural hierarchies and forecasts are not presented as current facts. Agreement with Cook is not a grading requirement. The final project is assessed for interpretation, source use, distinctions between claims and a reasoned revision.

Integration and verification

The builder generates 48 lesson pages and two indexes, links both homepages and preserves the existing study-note schema with a separate foodwise/NN identity. No new analytics or submission service is added. The standard build imports Foodwise after Nutrition.

Automated checks passed for chapter coverage, both languages, exact quotation matches to OCR pages, source references, local links and anchors, single headings, three answer checks plus rubric, language partners, course cards and saved-note controls. Existing Nutrition, guided-study, practice, foundation and passage-study checks also passed. The complete build encountered transient OneDrive write locks; it completed using a local retry wrapper without changing the shared build logic. Total output: 519 HTML pages including the existing learning review, twelve primary course cards and 214 paired guided-study lesson identities (meditation and foundations use separate routes).

Browser verification covered the course index, Chapter 7, paired Portuguese navigation, original and translated quotations, an expanded answer, completion marking and note persistence after reload. Temporary test notes were deleted and saving returned to its previous off state. The Portuguese lesson was checked at a 390 × 844 viewport with no horizontal overflow; the temporary viewport was reset afterwards.
