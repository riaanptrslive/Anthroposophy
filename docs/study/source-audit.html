Course library source audit — 17 September 2026

This audit checks what is available now, rather than assuming that an old source-register entry means the original file is still present. It covers the books and lecture sources behind the existing courses, plus the supplied Francis Edmunds example. It does not add unrelated books merely because they are in Downloads.

Findings that change the work

  1. A filename is wrong. The 20-page How to know Higher Worlds.pdf opens with The Essential Philosophy of Freedom: Third Expanded Edition, copyright Frederick Amrine 2022, and ends with capture-software screens. It is not the requested GA 10 source. This was checked from the PDF page images, not guessed from file size.
  2. According to Luke is incomplete. Its 20-page PDF contains front matter, McDermott's introduction and the beginning of Lecture 1; later pages contain software screens. The table of contents lists ten lectures and an editorial outline, but those entries are not their texts.
  3. The Foundation Stone booklet stops before its main text. Its eight pages include the contents, introduction and Steiner's explanatory note. The contents promise three translations and original German, but the final capture ends before those texts. The existing course's separate study translation cannot stand in for a comparative reading of this booklet.
  4. Encountering the Self is not currently available as an original file in the searched locations. Its retained 194-marker extraction and editorial work survive, including known gaps. The similarly themed On the threshold of adolecence.pdf is a different title and has not been substituted.
  5. Several saved extraction files are empty. The .sources/passage-sources files named colour, foundation, freedom, higher-worlds, luke, start, temperaments and weekly contain page records but no usable text. The Theosophy and Ancient Myths extractions do contain text. This is a distinction between extraction failure and a missing book.
  6. Some retained parallel-source paragraph files are in German. The files named think/thinking and temperaments are not English transcriptions despite their surrounding English-source workflow. They must be labelled by their actual language; English quotations need the correct source.
  7. Different page counts can be legitimate. Childs's current PDF has 97 multi-column captures; the previous text had 239 markers. Its checked ending reaches Appendix II and back matter. The smaller count does not establish that chapters are missing, but requires a new page alignment.
  1. The fuller Philosophy of Freedom capture lacks opening material. It starts partway through the 1918 preface. All fourteen chapters, conclusion, appendix and notes are present, but title/imprint, foreword, translation introduction and the preface beginning need restoration. The separate abridgement cannot supply the missing edition-specific pages.

Books versus courses

The library index keeps separate records for twenty source works/reference volumes. This number includes Edmunds as the supplied example and the online Practical Thinking lecture. It is not a claim that the website contains twenty primary courses.

  • The foundation and human-constitution pages synthesize reviewed material; they are not additional source books.
  • The unified Temperaments route draws on The Four Temperaments, The Mystery of Temperaments, and Childs. Each requires its own source record before comparison.
  • Meditation draws on Start Now!, Weekly Meditations, and The Foundation Stone Meditation. Selection in a shared course does not amount to complete reading of all three.
  • GA 225's What Is Anthroposophy? is distinct from Edmunds's An Introduction to Anthroposophy.
  • The Essential Philosophy of Freedom is an abridged/editorially expanded witness of the GA 4 material, not an unrelated second philosophy course.
  • Books named in a bibliography are not treated as read merely because the course mentions them.

New OCR has finished for all ten queued image-based sources (956 capture pages). This makes them readable for the next passes; it is not a claim that all 956 pages have been analyzed.

What is complete and what remains

Complete in this update: source-availability audit; identification of the misleading filename and missing sections; adoption of the supplied method; working record for each source; consolidation of existing English lesson material; a fresh detailed Theosophy full-text reading pass, covering all chapters, prefaces, introduction and thirteen addenda, with 80 explained units, fifteen dossiers, 33 lesson candidates and a coverage ledger. Theosophy’s final lessons remain to be expanded. The Philosophy of Freedom now also has a fresh supplied-text pass across both editions, with 108 explanation units, all fourteen chapters and both sets of appendices. The Four Temperaments has a complete lecture record and six English study sessions explaining 23 mapped units.

Not complete: a new deep sequential reading of every book; a complete library-wide highlight census (Theosophy and both Freedom captures have contact review; The Four Temperaments lecture pages have individual review); all twelve cumulative indexes for every book; new final bilingual course revisions. Working record headings explicitly identify pending work. The existing teaching bank is preserved for reuse, not relabelled as newly verified source extraction.

Release rule: source research comes before new final lessons. This update does not claim to have republished or replaced the website courses. It preserves their current addresses and teaching while the deeper pass proceeds.
