About the edition and source coverage

The Philosophy of Freedom / The Basis of Freedom · Rudolf Steiner; Wilson translation and Amrine editorial adaptation

147-page fuller capture and 70-page Amrine Essential 2022 capture freshly read, including all chapters, additions and six Essential appendices. The fuller file begins partway through the 1918 preface and lacks title/imprint, foreword and translation introduction. All-page contact review found no obvious coloured text highlights.

This curriculum uses the existing chapter analyses. It does not certify a new complete reading or fill a missing source with a different edition without attribution.

How the sources work together

The complete argument: Rudolf Steiner’s book in the Wilson translation supplies the chapter sequence and the fuller objections and 1918 additions. “Basis” means the supplied 147-marker study transcription; its pagination is not universal. Its front matter is incomplete. Use chapter headings in your own edition.

A concise reading and vocabulary aid: Frederick Amrine’s The Essential Philosophy of Freedom, Third Expanded Edition (2022), abridges the same underlying work and adds editorial material. It supports comparison; omissions are checked against the fuller book. “Representation” and Wilson’s “mental picture” need to be read in context.

Explanation through activity: Brian’s supplied lecture reviews inform the pauses, questions and worked examples. Commentary is checked against the book, not treated as another version of the argument. The Chapter 12 recording’s speaker was not independently identified. Original lessons do not reproduce or embed recordings.

Wannamaker’s selections and later Steiner excerpts in Amrine’s appendices are optional contextual sources. They are not Brian’s words or substitutes for the argument here. Portuguese explanations are original course text.
