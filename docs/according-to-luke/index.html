Sources and method

The supplied capture contains the introduction and part of Lecture 1. Complete readings use the parallel 1964 English edition; locate by date rather than page. Portuguese explanations are original course material.

The file is According to Luke: The Gospel of Compassion and Love Revealed, with an introduction by Robert A. McDermott. The supplementary edition is The Gospel of St. Luke, translated by Dorothy S. Osmond and Owen Barfield. The 1964 edition marks an omission at Lecture 9, paragraph 5; the editorial review also consulted the Steiner Online Library parallel version there.

Examples and exercises are teaching inventions. We distinguish the Gospel text, Steiner’s spiritual interpretation, the editorial introduction and the learner’s assessment. Understanding does not require agreement.
