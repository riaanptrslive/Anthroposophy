Comparisons added by the course author

These exact passages were selected for checking and study. Some locate passages in books the speaker names; others are additional comparisons. An attribution to a spiritual-research text is not independent historical or scientific verification of its claims.

GA 152 · The Four Sacrifices of Christ, 1 June 1914
Related Steiner primary text, not named by the speaker. Its sequence discusses sense organs, vital organs, and the harmony of thinking, feeling and willing before the earthly event. Compare it with the talk’s compressed walking–speaking–thinking scheme rather than treating them as a verbatim match.

Genesis 37 · Joseph’s dreams
Verses 5–11 describe sheaves and the sun, moon and eleven stars. The cow imagery in the transcript’s retelling belongs to Pharaoh’s dream in Genesis 41. This comparison uses the Knox translation.

Genesis 41 · Pharaoh’s dreams and Joseph’s responsibility
The cows belong to Pharaoh’s dream. Joseph interprets the dreams and proposes food storage; this chapter does not make him Pharaoh’s soothing musician.

1 Samuel 16:14–23 · David and Saul
David playing for Saul is a likely source of the musician motif in the transcript. This is an editorial inference about a possible conflation, not a claim about what the speaker intended.

Genesis 15:5 · the stars and descendants
The passage presents the multitude of descendants. The speaker’s twelve-constellation reading adds an esoteric interpretation; it should not erase the plain numerical comparison.

Genesis 30:21 · Dinah
Jacob’s twelve sons are not his only children. The narrative also names his daughter Dinah.

Genesis 14:18 · Melchizedek
Bread and wine, Salem and priesthood are in the narrative. The identity “Sun initiate” is the speaker’s esoteric interpretation.

Part 2 vocabulary

Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon
Earlier cosmic embodiments in this cosmology. Distinguish Old Moon from the present Moon, and all three from subdivisions of the present Earth embodiment.
Polaria, Hyperborea, Lemuria, Atlantis, post-Atlantis
The first five great Earth periods in the speaker’s sevenfold scheme. Two future periods are left unnamed. These are esoteric stages, not a geological timescale.
Recapitulation
An earlier stage being repeated or reworked within a later development, in the speaker’s account.
Elohim / Exusiai / Spirits of Form
Equivalent terms in the speaker’s hierarchy scheme. Six are associated with the Sun; Yahweh/Jehovah is associated with the present Moon.
Lucifer / Ahriman
Spiritual adversarial powers as understood in this talk. The speaker connects them with one-sided enthusiasm and hardening or control, while also discussing capacities he attributes to them.
Pre-earthly deeds
Christ’s preparatory activity before earthly incarnation, in the speaker’s account. Here “pre-earthly” does not mean before the Earth existed.
Sister soul of Adam / heavenly Adam
The preserved spiritual soul that the speaker associates with Luke’s Jesus. “Sister soul” does not by itself identify Eve or prove the Mary hypothesis.
Ancient cultural periods
The speaker’s esoteric India, Persia and other periods are not interchangeable with the documented histories or the diversity of present-day religions and peoples.
“Primal Semites” and other Atlantean labels
Historical terminology used in the lecture and its source tradition. Preserve it for attribution, not as a current biological taxonomy or a ranking of human worth.
Michael / folk soul
The speaker connects spiritual guidance of a people with an archangel and describes Michael’s later elevation to the Archai. His precise source attribution is still to be located.
