Phases: Understanding the Course of a Life

How does a life keep developing? Explore changing roles, relationships and purpose through the seven chapters of Phases.

Begin the course →

What this book is about

Lievegoed reads adult biography through three interacting dimensions: body, inner experience and spiritual direction. A recurring theme, or leitmotiv, can take new forms as life changes. Age phases offer approximate landmarks for asking questions about that development.

How you will learn

Each lesson explains the ideas before asking you to work with a checked quotation, an example, an activity and three questions with explained answers. Allow 25–40 minutes plus reading time. Split the longer book sections across several sittings.

Use your copy for the full reading. You may use an invented biography in every activity. Assessment rewards accurate reading and reasoned interpretation; personal disclosure and agreement with the author are optional. The final portfolio draws on all seven chapters.

Your learning outcomes

  • Read life transitions without rigid age deadlines.
  • Recognize relationships, orientations and learning needs.
  • Compare explanatory models and their limits.
  • Develop a biographical interpretation with an open future.

An adult reading course; no prior course is required.
