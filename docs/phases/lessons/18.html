Understand the chapter

The key ideas, explained

The symphony image brings the course together. A biography includes distinct movements and a recurring theme, but also interruptions and changes of direction. Coherence is something to investigate and compose; a neat story should not erase the events that challenge it.

Use Chapter 1 to distinguish types of development; Chapter 2 to examine transitions; Chapter 3 to consider relationships; Chapter 4 to notice orientations; Chapter 5 to connect learning and work; Chapter 6 to identify explanatory assumptions and boundaries of help; and Chapter 7 to formulate a practice and next step.

A strong portfolio distinguishes events, the book’s concepts and your own interpretation. It offers an alternative reading and an open question. The future proposal should be modest enough to try, with a review point that allows learning to change the plan.

A passage to read closely

The human biography is a symphony which each individual personally composes

Bernard Lievegoed · Phases · Chapter 7 heading · PDF capture page 130.

Read the wording

“Composes” emphasizes participation. It does not mean a person causes every event or controls other people’s actions.

A worked example

A fictional portfolio proposes “creating spaces for learning” as its theme. It includes a period when financial necessity, rather than that theme, determined the person’s work.

Keeping the difficult fit visible makes the interpretation more credible. The next step might be a small teaching project, with time and income constraints recorded.

Three key takeaways

  • Integrate all seven chapters without forcing agreement.
  • Separate evidence, source concepts and interpretation.
  • Leave the future and the reading open to revision.
