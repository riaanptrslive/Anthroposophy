Sources and method

Source: Colour, translated by John Salter (Lectures 1–3) and Pauline Wehrle (4–12), Rudolf Steiner Press. Page assignments refer to the supplied capture markers, not another edition’s pagination. Explanations and activities are original.

The collection contains three GA 291 lectures and nine drawn from other volumes. Locate readings by date and the assigned pages. The public index below helps locate parallel editions; their text and pagination may differ.

We distinguish observation, personal response, artistic proposal and spiritual interpretation. Understanding does not require agreement. Digital studies are original teaching illustrations: screens and materials affect their appearance. Practical activities do not include the treatments proposed in Lecture 8.

Bring this study into everyday life

Develop observation and artistic judgment through relationships between colours.

At home

Make a small colour study with available materials. Describe what you see and feel before deciding what the colours mean.

As an educator

Offer room for an image to develop through colour. Ask learners about their decisions rather than requiring identical results.
