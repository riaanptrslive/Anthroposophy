Learn the ideas before applying them

Sources and method

Reading references use the supplied 32-page capture. Its translator and edition are unidentified; repeated and damaged passages are not reproduced. The linked Dawson translation is a parallel reading, catalogued as Karlsruhe, 19 January 1909, GA 68d, not a verified identification of this edition. Lessons and practical adaptations are original.

The main text occupies pages 2–30; pages 31–32 are advertisements. Bodily correspondences and historical educational proposals are studied with attribution. Activities use fictional situations, attainable challenges and voluntary care.
