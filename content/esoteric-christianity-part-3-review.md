## Located sources and corrections

- [Deeper Secrets, Appendix I](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/DeepSecrets/DepSec_appendix1.html) locates the two-dangers passage. The speaker correctly identifies it as a compilation in the appendix; it must not be attributed as verbatim wording from a dated Steiner lecture. The selected lectures are from November 1909, not 1999.
- [Lecture II, 9 November 1909](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/DeepSecrets/19091109p01.html) provides a comparison for Abraham and the preparation. [Lecture III, 23 November](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/DeepSecrets/19091123p01.html), especially paragraphs 13–15, contains Esau/Jacob and hair. These are attributed esoteric claims, not biology or a ranking of living populations.
- [Genesis 17:5](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/genesis/17) attributes Abram’s renaming to God. Genesis 25’s birthright and Genesis 27’s blessing deception are separate scenes. The speaker’s precise Melchizedek/Noah attribution remains unlocated.
- [Matthew 2:16](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/matthew/2) says two years old and under; the recording says under three. Family wealth and an exact seven-child household are not established by this chapter. The apparent reference to Abraham’s spirit at 00:43:33 is not used to invent an Abraham–Zarathustra identity.
- [GA 123, Lecture VI, 6 September 1910](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/Dates/19100906p01.html), paragraph 19, locates Mathai, Netzer and the Nazareth account. Bibliographic verification does not establish that account archaeologically.
- [GA 114, Lecture II, 16 September 1909](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA114/English/SOL/19090916p01.html), paragraph 42, identifies Simeon with Asita visiting the infant Bodhisattva. The speaker’s retelling associates the earlier figure with Gautama after leaving home; the difference is explicit in Lesson 28.
- [Luke 2](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/luke/2) distinguishes the infant presentation, verses 22–38, from the visit at twelve, verses 41–52. The latter is not in Luke 3. Anna remains in the course even though the speaker develops Simeon’s interpretation more fully.
- [The Bhagavad Gita and the West](https://shop.rsarchive.org/products/the-bhagavad-gita-and-the-west) is identified as the 2009 composite volume of GA 142 and GA 146, with a GA 139 appendix, McDermott introduction and Easwaran translation. Exact speaker edition unconfirmed. [GA 142, Lecture V, 1 January 1913](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA142/English/AP1971/19130101p01.html), paragraphs 51–61, is a selected comparison for the preserved soul, transfer, baptism and Krishna. The translation’s distinctions among appearance, incorporation and incarnation prevent a simplistic “never appeared before” claim.
- [GA 148, 6 October 1913](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/Dates/19131006p01.html), particularly paragraphs 18–20, provides a comparison for the conversation, withdrawal, baptism and two mothers. This supports a two-mother account within Steiner’s teaching, not proof of the lecturer’s Eve identities. The mother’s transformation is not described by the course as biological restoration.
- [GA 109, 31 May 1909](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/19090531p01.html), paragraph 34, is an editorial comparison not named as a book by the speaker. It assigns astral copies to both Francis of Assisi and Thomas Aquinas, and an etheric copy to Augustine. The speaker’s “I think” about an etheric copy for Aquinas is explicitly corrected.
- [From Jesus to Christ, GA 131](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA131/) is the new closing recommendation. It is identified bibliographically, while the recording’s phantom discussion remains an introduction. No claim is made to have completed an independent reading of the entire cycle for this extension.
