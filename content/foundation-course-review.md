# Foundation and book-course organization

The approved revision creates an 18-lesson introductory route, not an 18-lesson limit on the library. The full ten course paths and three optional temperament source companions remain accessible. Existing book lesson URLs and browser notebook identities have not changed.

Foundation lessons introduce observation and interpretation; body, soul and spirit; thinking, feeling and willing; the fourfold account in two steps; bodily threefoldness; comparison of classifications; differentiated inner life; individuality; memory; freedom; reincarnation; karma; soul and spiritual worlds; spiritual knowledge; and synthesis. Each page offers connected explanation, a source direction, one question with feedback, and optional deeper reading. The orientation distinguishes Steiner’s work, later authors and course adaptation, with a brief historical context.

The foundation curates existing original bilingual Theosophy teaching and the reviewed concept explanations, with new teaching on observation, bodily threefoldness, spiritual cognition and synthesis. It is not a replacement translation of the source books. New source checks used GA 301, 21 April 1920, for bodily threefoldness; GA 12 for spiritual cognition; and the Archive’s biographical timeline for orientation. Specific physiological claims in GA 301 are not presented as contemporary scientific findings.

The homepage separates deeper books, accompanying Practical Thinking, and optional subjects. Theosophy’s complete 23-lesson index moves to `/theosophy/index.html` in each language; the old homepage `#lessons` anchor remains a route to that index. All ten course indexes link to relevant foundation lessons. The former repeated long primers are shortened to contextual reminders with the full reference still available.

New foundation exercises use a personal notebook, with no new browser-storage mechanism. Existing course note controls remain unchanged. Build with `node scripts/build-all.mjs`; check with `node scripts/check-foundation-course.mjs` and all existing check scripts. The complete site contains 439 HTML pages, including the existing editorial review.
