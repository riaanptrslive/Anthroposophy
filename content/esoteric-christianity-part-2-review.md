## Decisions preserving source fidelity

1. Keep Old Moon separate from the present Moon; keep cosmic embodiments, great Earth periods and post-Atlantean cultures on separate levels.
2. Attribute cosmological dates, flood duration, heredity, body/brain development and ancestral classifications to the speaker. Do not treat them as established natural science or as measures of living peoples. Historical racial labels are retained only to identify the argument being studied.
3. The Mary/heavenly-Eve identification remains personal conjecture. Part 2's repetition does not upgrade it into verified Steiner teaching. At 00:40:07–00:40:18 the speaker again acknowledges the absence of a direct description of the relationship.
4. Retain uncertainty in Michael's exact attribution and Zarathustra's rank. Retain the alternation between approximately 3½ and 3⅓ years without manufacturing precision.
5. The pineal/pituitary question is unanswered. No physiological explanation is added.
6. The political-violence question is included as a contextual audience question and the answer as a spiritual interpretation. The unnamed opponent of physical form is not silently named. The lesson's practical activity concerns ordinary decisions, not diagnosing people.
7. Distinguish the biblical three groups of fourteen from the speaker's physical/etheric/astral interpretation. Separate biographical years from generations where the transcript slips between units.
8. Genesis comparisons: Melchizedek's Sun identity is an added reading; the star promise's multitude is not erased by the zodiac reading; twelve sons does not exhaust Jacob's children. Hagar's situation is not reduced to the speaker's dismissive characterisation.
9. Joseph's dreams in [Genesis 37](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen037.htm) concern sheaves and heavenly bodies; cows belong to Pharaoh's dream in [Genesis 41](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/genesis/41). The musician motif resembles [David and Saul, 1 Samuel 16](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1samuel/16); that resemblance is identified as an editorial inference about possible conflation. Without audio, do not decide whether the mismatch originated with the speaker or transcription.
10. Broad statements about Egyptian incapacity and the religious paths of whole cultures stay attributed and open to question. The educational focus is the proposed relation between perception and practical judgment.
