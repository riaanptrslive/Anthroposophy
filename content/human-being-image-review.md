### Fourfold prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset: original watercolour illustration for a bilingual beginner course about Rudolf Steiner's fourfold human being. Landscape 3:2 format on warm ivory cotton paper, restrained sage, blue-violet, ochre and terracotta, soft wet-on-wet pigment and carefully readable forms. Four equally sized vignettes arranged as a spacious 2 by 2 grid, separated by generous ivory gutters, no borders. Top left: a plainly visible human hand gently holding a smooth stone, material bodily contact. Top right: a living bean plant with leaves growing from soil, formative life. Bottom left: an attentive human face responding to the scent of a flower, inward sensory experience, understated expression. Bottom right: a seated adult thoughtfully considering a small open notebook, individual reflection. Vignettes are teaching examples of four different questions, not images of invisible bodies; do not depict energy shells, auras, chakras, anatomy, cosmic rays, ghost bodies, halos or occult symbols. No text, letters, labels, numbers, logos or watermark. These are original teaching illustrations, not reproductions of Steiner's drawings. Calm sophisticated hand-painted watercolour, clear at small sizes.

### Threefold prompt

Original educational watercolour, landscape 3:2 on warm ivory paper. Three spacious equal vertical vignettes across the page in gentle sage green, ochre, terracotta, blue. Same adult woman in same garden with a flowering rose bush in each vignette. Left: she carefully touches a rose petal while looking at the flower, bodily sensory contact. Middle: she pauses with a quiet delighted expression beside the flower, personal inward response. Right: she carefully compares two different roses and their leaves, attentive thoughtful expression, seeking relationships beyond personal preference. These are three aspects of one encounter, not three separate bodies. Hand painted soft wet-on-wet watercolour with clear realistic hands and readable natural gestures, uncluttered ivory gutters. No text, letters, numbers, labels, borders, aura, ghost, halo, chakra or mystical rays. For a beginner lesson explaining body, soul and spirit in Steiner's Theosophy; original course illustration, never a reproduction of Steiner's own artwork.
